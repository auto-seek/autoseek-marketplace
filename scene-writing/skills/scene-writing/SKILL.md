---
name: scene-writing
description: Design or refine a short interactive scene for an existing team when the user asks for a setting, character voice or conversation starter.
---

Start from the user’s current team and the actual members they name. Propose one concrete setting, one situation worth discussing and two optional conversation starters. Keep every participant’s established identity and work responsibilities.

Give characters distinct priorities and ways of speaking without making any character hostile to the user. Use concise, natural dialogue and leave room for the user to choose what happens next. Separate a proposed line of dialogue from a message that was actually sent.

When editing a character card, preserve attribution and unknown fields and describe the specific personality or example-dialogue change. Do not change the member’s model, permissions or active task merely to fit the scene. Send a group message only when the user asks you to send it, using the host’s actual group tool if available.
