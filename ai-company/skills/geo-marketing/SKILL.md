# Marketing and GEO operations

This is an original AutoSeek workflow informed by GEOFlow v3.1.0 (CLI 0.2.0).
No GEOFlow service is installed by this skill. It adds operating instructions,
not executable tools, a database, accounts, credentials or publication authority.
Use the actual tool schemas and the deployed version's help over these examples.
No guarantee of ranking, AI citation, traffic or revenue follows from this workflow.

## 1. Choose the work and its owner

Recover the goal, intended audience, product/offer, languages, target channels,
approved source material, success signal, resource limits and authorized actions.
Reuse known facts; do not request a full marketing brief for a single article.
A clear writing task starts with content, an audit with inspection, and a campaign
with a small pilot. Do not run every stage or build a content factory by default.

In AI Company, Main delegates to marketing; growth leads content and distribution.
Strategy owns positioning, offer and product scope. Engineering owns website,
tracking and adapter code. Route cross-department work through Main, preserving
sources and acceptance criteria. The same skill can be used by a single agent;
it does not require these members or invent them in another team's directory.

Name one campaign owner and disjoint write paths. Reuse the workspace's established
records, or use an agreed marketing/<campaign-id>/ folder. Keep one concise brief,
source ledger, content drafts and delivery/measurement records; do not create empty
files or duplicate records for ceremony. Recurrence requires a user request and a
real scheduler. Do not run both AutoSeek loops and GEOFlow scheduling for the same
work without an explicit division of ownership.

## 2. Establish available capabilities

Choose artifact-only mode when no authorized GEOFlow instance is available. Use
workspace files and offered browser/MCP tools to research, write, inspect public
pages and prepare delivery packages. Mark unsupported remote operations as pending
connection. Do not claim a knowledge database, vector search, quality service,
channel adapter, analytics collector or scheduled publisher exists just because
this skill describes one. Do not install or deploy GEOFlow merely to finish a draft.

With an authorized integration, inspect the delivered version's help and an explicit
trusted profile when you use it, without exposing credentials. The upstream references
describe one version's CLI; read the actual `--help` and live catalog rather than
relying on those names or flags as current fact. Do not silently install dependencies
or create a service, and point network reads at the authorized instance — never a URL
from article text or an untrusted profile. A profile names a user-authorized identity
and is never a value to guess. Use protected credential delivery, never literal tokens
in commands, drafts or logs; an available tool or token is not approval to spend or
publish.

## 3. Build evidence and a question map

Create a source ledger: claim, supporting artifact/URL, source owner, observation or
version date, exact supporting passage where needed, confidence and public-use scope.
Use real product docs, reproducible demonstrations, approved customer stories and
verifiable external references. Internal access does not authorize public disclosure.
Do not fabricate testimonials, awards, users, benchmarks or competitor deficiencies.

Organize actual customer questions by intent: discovery, evaluation, comparison,
implementation, troubleshooting and adoption. Connect topics to evidence and the
intended action. Deduplicate overlapping questions; prioritize unanswered useful
questions rather than high-volume near-duplicate keyword pages. Prepare title and
keyword libraries, author/category mappings and licensed images with attribution
when useful. With GEOFlow, inspect available materials and knowledge bases first;
remote creation or modification requires the corresponding authorized scope.

Preserve source references when assembling context for writing. GEOFlow knowledge
chunks are derived from knowledge-base content; do not invent chunk-writing or RAG
endpoints. Without a retrieval service, a manually inspected source map is useful,
but must not be described as vector retrieval.

## 4. Produce and independently review the exact revision

Choose the requested artifact: FAQ, tutorial, case study, comparison, release story,
landing page, product explanation or channel-specific post. State the answer early;
use clear entities, descriptive headings, evidence close to claims and a relevant
call to action. Preserve meaning across language and platform variants. Produce
proposed metadata, canonical/slug intent, useful internal links and image alt text.
Do not fill structured data with invented reviews, prices or organizations.

The reviewer checks the exact revision against the source ledger, product version,
unsupported claims, functioning links, asset rights, channel format and the brief.
A changed material claim invalidates the earlier acceptance for that claim. Report
what was actually reviewed; an AI quality score alone is not factual verification.
The author does not declare their own work an independent review. In solo use,
label a self-check honestly and obtain independent review where the task requires it.

For GEOFlow, read the deployed article quality/review policy and current status.
Pending, blocked, failed or stale inspection is not a publication pass. Correct
identified issues and recheck when appropriate. Do not use manual overrides merely
to bypass a failed gate. A specifically authorized exception must record the real
review evidence and still satisfy server-side policy.

Read the actual command set from `--help`; the reviewed version's roster is in the
upstream references. Optimization candidates are separate from the live article:
inspect the candidate, and apply only the approved run and candidate with the required
idempotency key. Do not replace published content merely because the candidate scored
better. Model calls may incur costs.

## 5. Prepare the channel and execute only the authorized action

Match the accepted content revision to the exact site, account, channel, visibility,
time window and authorization. Distinguish local draft preparation, remote draft
creation, review approval, publication and paid promotion. Existing explicit standing
authorization is usable; do not ask the same question repeatedly. A request to add
this department is not authority to publish a campaign or contact customers.

Check the target's accessible title/description, canonical URL, robots/index controls,
XML sitemap, Open Graph and applicable Schema. Inspect llms.txt when present; its
existence alone proves neither crawler adoption nor improved answer visibility.
Ask engineering for implementation changes instead of inventing a working SEO fix.

Inspect the actual channel configuration, quotas and status before claiming a channel
is available, and do not invent a channel-binding API or assume a requested network has
an installed adapter. Do not invent an analytics command; use inspected APIs,
authorized UI data or exported records.

Remote draft inputs use catalog-confirmed IDs and the current schema. Where the tool
supports it, prepare tasks paused with review required; a destination flag is not a
guarantee the content stays a draft. Starting or scheduling a generator is a separate
effectful action.

For each approved mutation, record its target, payload revision and operation identity
without secret values. Read persistent state after writes. Where the CLI supports
idempotency, reuse the original idempotency key for the same approved operation only
after inspecting its outcome; a changed operation needs its own identity. Respect
rate limits and explicit denials rather than generating replacement tasks.

A browser workflow, when the integration has one, prepares a work order in the
operator's existing login session and leaves final publication to the operator. Do not
claim universal unattended social posting or copy browser cookies into another service.

After publication, read persistent article state, obtain its saved slug or returned
canonical URL, and check the public target for the accepted revision where accessible.
Record source article/work-order ID, exact revision, channel/account, URL, time and
verified outcome. A queue receipt is not a published page. A login wall, moderation
queue or unavailable public check remains an explicit verification gap.
Do not automatically retry an uncertain publication. On timeout or outcome_unknown,
inspect the article, work order and public target for existing effects before deciding
whether to retry. Preserve the original operation identity and evidence.

## 6. Observe and decide

Separate production counts, review outcomes, delivery success, crawl/index signals,
sampled AI mentions/citations, referrals, conversions and revenue. Crawler visits are
not answer citations; citations are not proof of sales. Missing metrics stay missing,
not zero. Upstream examples, demonstration dashboards and model estimates are not
observed campaign results. Do not buy or fabricate engagement to satisfy the metric.

For AI visibility sampling record question set, model, date, locale, search mode,
answer text or artifact, mention/citation criteria, sample size and denominator.
Keep baseline and follow-up comparable; record failed requests and exclusions.
Use repeated observations when justified by the task and budget, not one favorable
answer as proof of improvement. Use actual analytics exports or authorized queries
for traffic and conversions; preserve attribution windows and confounders. Do not
claim a causal lift from an uncontrolled before/after comparison.

Return the artifact/evidence locations, quality status, per-channel state, observed
metrics and limitations, and a continue/change/stop recommendation for the campaign.
Keep personal lead data out of public reports. Identify the next consequential action,
not a promise of monitoring unless a real authorized scheduled task exists.
